# HAMSTER

> The operating system for service businesses.

---

# Vision

HAMSTER helps service businesses run their business from the tools they already use.

Instead of forcing businesses to adopt another complicated CRM, Hamster works alongside existing communication channels—starting with WhatsApp—to automate bookings, customer management, payments, reminders, and business operations.

Our goal is simple:

> Turn WhatsApp into the operating system for service businesses.

---

# Mission

Remove repetitive work from business owners.

Every booking.

Every reminder.

Every customer follow-up.

Every appointment.

Every payment reminder.

Every business insight.

Should eventually be handled automatically by HAMSTER.

---

# Product Philosophy

HAMSTER is not another booking application.

HAMSTER is an AI Business Operator.

The software should feel like hiring another employee—not installing another dashboard.

Every feature should answer one question:

"Does this reduce work for the business owner?"

If not, it shouldn't exist.

# Product Evolution

## v1 — Operations Engine

Run the business.

Features include:

- bookings
- customers
- services
- calendar
- reminders
- invoices

---

## v1.1 — WhatsApp Engine

Operate directly from WhatsApp.

The AI understands customer messages and performs business actions automatically.

Examples:

Customer:

"I need haircut tomorrow 3pm."

↓

AI creates booking.

↓

Checks availability.

↓

Saves customer.

↓

Sends confirmation.

---

## v2 — Growth Engine

Help businesses grow.

Examples:

- marketing campaigns
- promotions
- customer segmentation
- reactivation campaigns
- referrals
- reviews

---

## v3 — Intelligence Engine

An AI employee.

The AI should proactively help the owner.

Examples:

"Tuesday afternoons are always empty."

"You should increase your haircut price."

"You haven't contacted these customers in 90 days."

"You may run out of inventory next week."

HAMSTER becomes the AI operating system that runs service businesses.
